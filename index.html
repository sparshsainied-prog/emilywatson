Hello, I’m Emily

I grew up in the United Arab Emirates and Singapore before my family settled in Sugar Land, Texas. I studied history at the University of Houston's Honors College.

When I'm not writing, I'm a craftaholic: bookbinding, mapmaking, sewing and whatever else I can get my hands on. My friends often find me lost in the woods.

I seek to honour Jesus and to pursue His glory wherever it may take me.
